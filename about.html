Meet the owner

JJ Przybylski.

JJ founded Mastodon Moving in 2011 after years in the moving business, setting out to build a company that not only provided unrivaled customer service but also did its part to change the world around it for the better. Following the mantra to "be the change you want to see in the world," Mastodon strives to raise the standard of professionalism, customer service, and social responsibility in the moving industry.

"People rely on us to move their prized possessions and valuables. It's our job to give clarity, the best customer service, and the best moving services to ensure a stress-free move." — JJ Przybylski, founder

Today, Mastodon Moving is stronger than ever and continues to move people into their new homes with care, while actively supporting socially responsible initiatives. JJ received his degree in business management from Fairfield University in 2013.

Who we are

Our commitment

Mastodon Moving LLC is a fully licensed and insured small business founded in Wayland, MA. We provide personal, high-quality customer service throughout the entire moving process — from your initial estimate onwards, you'll be in the hands of a small team committed to making your move as easy as possible.

Our experienced and friendly movers treat their profession with pride. They care about each customer and take every precaution to protect your home. We'll always make sure you're aware of your options for coverage, inventories, and storage. Fourteen years on, we operate out of a 34,000 sq ft warehouse in Westborough with a team of full-time movers, packers, and project managers — and most of our work still comes from referrals.

Doing it greener

Environmental
care.

How we move today

Using renewable resources is important to us. Everything that doesn't fit into our recyclable cardboard boxes gets wrapped in reusable pads, and we prioritize rubber bands over tape when wrapping furniture.

We also make sure to recycle all materials at the appropriate facilities — for electronic disposals, we work with Computer Recycling USA to lower our environmental impact.

Looking toward the future

In our quest to lower our carbon footprint even further, we're actively researching methods and equipment to cut our fleet's fuel dependency. It's our hope that it inspires other companies — and individuals — to join us in our environmental efforts.
