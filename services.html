Contents process
after a loss.

Fire, water, or wind — a loss is disruptive, and getting back to normal starts with moving salvageable contents off site while the rebuild takes place. Mastodon is one call for damage determination, pack-out, cleaning, and storage.

Not everything is lost. Many items that look ruined — smoke-damaged china, soaked documents, soot-covered furniture and antiques — can be cleaned and recovered to pre-loss condition at our facility, and returned to you when the rebuild is done.

Our comprehensive inventory service streamlines the claims process for policyholders and adjusters alike, with accurate documentation from the first walkthrough to the pack-back.

  • Fire & smoke: pack-out, cleaning, and deodorization to pre-loss condition
  • Water & sewer: fast removal to reduce exposure and speed remediation
  • 360° photo and video capture with Ricoh ThetaX + Encircle software
  • Non-salvageable inventory with brands, ages, and values for the claim
  • Fire & smoke damage certified technicians on staff
